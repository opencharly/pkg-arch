# 2026.227.0743 — docs: adopt the Agent-in-the-Loop Factory tagline

The product positioning line changed. OpenCharly is now **The Agent-in-the-Loop
Factory**, and the tagline sentence is "the fully equipped factory floor for you
and your agents". This repo advertised charly under two older wordings, in two
different places:

- `PKGBUILD:5` (`pkgdesc=`) carried the oldest of the three — "OpenCharly
  container management CLI — compose, build, deploy container boxes from
  configurable candies" — which predates both the kitchen tagline and the
  factory one. It now reads "OpenCharly CLI — the fully equipped factory floor
  for you and your agents: compose, build, deploy, and evaluate boxes from
  configurable candies".
- `README.md:3` carried the kitchen tagline adopted in `2026.217.2243` ("the
  fully stocked gourmet kitchen for you and your agents"); it now carries the
  factory one.

Description text only. Neither `depends`, `optdepends`, `makedepends`, nor any
build/package function is touched — the superproject's
`charly/packaging_test.go` greps this PKGBUILD for dependency parity against
the embedded packaging section, and that parity is unchanged
(`TestPackagingSectionWellFormed` + `TestPackagingSectionCompleteness` pass).

## Why `.SRCINFO` was hand-edited rather than regenerated

`.SRCINFO`'s `pkgdesc` was updated by hand to the byte-identical PKGBUILD value
instead of by `makepkg --printsrcinfo`, because a full regeneration on this repo
today rewrites three things that have nothing to do with the tagline:

- `source =` gains the **generating machine's absolute path** plus a
  `#commit=<sha>` pin for both the superproject and the sdk submodule. The
  PKGBUILD derives its sources from `$startdir/../..` at parse time, so whatever
  checkout runs the regeneration gets baked into the committed file — the
  current committed value (`/home/atrawog/Sync/Atrapub/av/charly`) is already
  one such stale artifact from an earlier machine.
- `depends` gains `tmux`, which `2026.215.1737` added to the PKGBUILD without a
  matching `.SRCINFO` refresh.
- `pkgver` moves `2026.182.0002` → `2026.198.1029`.

Those three are a real, pre-existing divergence between `PKGBUILD` and
`.SRCINFO`, and they are recorded here rather than fixed in passing: the fix is
not a regeneration (which re-bakes a machine-local path), it is deciding whether
a LOCAL-ONLY, never-published-to-the-AUR package should carry a checked-in
`.SRCINFO` at all — `.SRCINFO` exists to serve the AUR RPC, and `README.md`
states this package is not published there. That decision belongs to its own
change.
